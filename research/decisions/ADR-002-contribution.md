# ADR-002 — provisional contribution after prior-art challenge

Date: 2026-09-25. Status: provisional until B03 data audit; no method or dataset frozen.

**Choice:** pursue H02-1 only as a grouped, source-scoped evaluation of NIR composition-proxy REVIEW decisions for a named historical criterion, with simple PLS/direct eligibility/fixed-threshold and an existing precision-control option as serious comparators. Report the observed false-acceptance/coverage tradeoff and uncertainty rather than a new theorem. Use H02-2, a reproduction and data-limitation study, if B03 finds too few independent threshold-relevant groups or inadequate labels. The dataset choice itself remains open between OpenTextile, NIST and any lawful accessible alternative.

**Why:** original OpenTextile authors already fit PLS on their samples (S50); textile NIR quantification and “Not classified” outcomes exist (S41–S44); T-REX and vendors already map NIR readings to recycler requirements (S35, S30); general selected-set risk methods and a binary precision controller exist (S39, S40, S15). B02 found no supported algorithmic novelty. NIST S37 could be a stronger controlled-blend dataset, but B03 must inspect the files and label provenance. A negative or inconclusive comparison is a legitimate outcome.

**Alternatives:** claim a new industrial sorter or formal guarantee — rejected by direct prior art and absent operator/independence evidence; train a deep model by default — deferred because classical PLS is strong and dataset size is unknown; switch immediately to NIST — deferred until file/license/group/boundary audit; choose only OpenTextile — rejected as premature; abandon textile scope entirely — unnecessary while a falsifiable domain evaluation remains possible.

**Decision reversal triggers:** B03 shows no source-labeled independent positive/negative specimens near a named profile threshold; labels or groups cannot be audited; a simple baseline suffices under the same decision budget; or prospective precision cannot be estimated meaningfully. Do not consume final-test outcomes to choose a winner. No tests, experiments or final labels were accessed in this block.
