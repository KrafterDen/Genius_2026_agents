# ADR-001 — narrow the problem after independent challenge

Date: 2026-09-25. Status: provisional pending B02 prior art and B03 data gates.

Decision: replace broad end-of-life textile routing and environmental-benefit wording with an evaluation of NIR-based, profile-specific *fibre-composition proxy* decisions on independent physical sample groups. Use 2024 JRC profiles only as historical scenarios and Infinited's ~88% cotton statement only as a public preference/sensitivity case. Require review for unobserved whole-item conditions. No operator certification or novelty claim.

Reasons: current equipment already estimates fibre fractions and applies thresholds (S30, S32); the candidate dataset is small, supplier-labelled and has provenance ambiguity (S06); JRC explicitly limits its criteria to a 2024 snapshot (S09). Simpler baselines and data adequacy can falsify usefulness. Record a negative or inconclusive result if they do.

Alternatives considered: (a) full industrial acceptance claim — rejected for absent operator contract and missing conditions; (b) generic dominant-fibre classification — retain as a baseline, not enough to answer profile thresholds; (c) no AI, manual/specification sorting — retain as a practical comparator where inputs are available; (d) new spectrometer/deep neural model — deferred pending data and resource evidence.
