| Objection | Primary evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| NIR fibre estimation and threshold routing are already deployed. | [Valvan Fibersort (S30)](https://www.valvan.com/en/solutions/textile-sorting-recycling) advertises NIR fibre-concentration prediction and RGB colour sorting; [Matoha FabriTell (S32)](https://matoha.com/configuring-the-material-indicator-led-fabritell) offers configurable material-percentage LED rules. | **Replace** any novelty claim based on NIR plus a threshold. B02 must identify a defensible evaluation or method gap against these and classical baselines; no gap is currently established. |
| Recycler composition criteria differ and are not whole-item acceptance. | [JRC Table 5 (S09)](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/bitstream/JRC141441/JRC141441_01.pdf) is a 2024 WRAP-derived snapshot; adjacent sections qualify colour, disruptors, layers and structure. [Infinited (S28–S29)](https://infinitedfiber.com/faq/) states an ~88% cotton preference, while [Recover (S31)](https://recoverfiber.com/faqs) says blends need case-specific review. | **Keep** only dated *composition proxy* scenarios. **Defer** operator acceptance, industrial purity and universal thresholds. |
| Dataset may not support threshold or population claims. | [VTT article (S06)](https://cris.vtt.fi/en/publications/introducing-opentextile-nir-near-infrared-hyperspectral-imaging-a/) describes 71 physical samples, supplier composition metadata, small garment pieces and contradictory post-industrial/used-garment wording. | **Test in B03:** inspect actual files/license, highest dependent group, class balance and near-threshold independent specimens. If insufficient, narrow or abandon profile-specific evaluation. |
| A simple rule may beat the proposed policy, and a supplier label is an oracle for the label-defined target. | [Matoha (S32)](https://matoha.com/configuring-the-material-indicator-led-fabritell) demonstrates simple thresholds; JRC and operator pages define different criteria. | **Test later:** use supplier-label threshold only as the proxy target/oracle, not as a deployable baseline unless the label is available at decision time. Compare majority/dominant-fibre, simple NIR spectral models, direct eligibility and review policies on grouped units under the same information budget. Do not tune using final data. |
| Environmental and human-labour benefits are not observed. | [T-REX period-2 report (S11)](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101060343/reporting) links sorting/preprocessing to feedstock quality but says impact depends on recycling process and energy; [FAE (S10)](https://www.fashionforgood.com/case-study/feedstock-activation-europe/) describes costly heterogeneous feedstock. | **Defer** avoided emissions, tonnes diverted, time saved and factory validation. The study may only report its measured proxy errors, review rate and resource use when those measurements exist. |

## Falsification plan, unexecuted
1. B03: audit actual metadata and source license. For each candidate profile, count independent physical groups clearly above, below and near the criterion, preserving uncertain/rounded compositions. No pixel-to-item inflation.
2. B02 and B06: identify closest selective/reject and NIR methods; compare a simple feature/threshold approach and classical spectra learner with any custom review rule. Specify evaluation before final data access.
3. Reject or narrow a profile if no independent threshold-relevant positives/negatives exist or source labels cannot define the target. If simple comparators achieve the same risk/coverage tradeoff, report no added value.

The B01 choice is **conditional sample-level fibre-composition eligibility relative to supplier metadata**, with explicit REVIEW for unobserved whole-item requirements. It is a research question and candidate scope, not a passed scientific result.
