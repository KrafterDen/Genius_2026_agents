# B02 independent alternatives challenge

The textile and selective-method tracks were separately assigned as read-only GPT-6 Sol Medium workers. Both explicit spawns were accepted; runtime model identities were not exposed. Root performed an additional cross-domain search, opened original T-REX, NIST, JMLR, MAPIE, OpenTextile and Bonifazi pages, and integrated the objections. No tests or dataset experiments were run.

The independent statistical challenger identified [cfBH (S39)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v24/22-1176.html) as especially close: expected false-discovery proportion among selected units under its exact score/exchangeability/independent-training/tie/fixed-target conditions. Marginal conformal coverage and per-unit expected loss are different estimands. With only 71 described physical OpenTextile samples before exclusions or splits, an advertised strong selective error bound may be impossible. B03/B04 must quantify the actual feasible statement rather than importing a theorem by name.

**Decision from challenge:** primary candidate H02-1 is a domain evaluation with no algorithmic novelty claim; H02-2 is a faithful reproduction/limitations fallback. If B03 cannot identify threshold-relevant independent units, abandon H02-1 and record an honest no-gap or no-data result. Details and bounded falsification conditions are in `research/candidate_hypotheses.json`.
