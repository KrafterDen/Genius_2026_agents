Two read-only native workers reviewed B04 independently: a requested GPT-6 Astra High statistical challenger and a GPT-6 Sol Medium evaluation-design reviewer. The root compared their objections with original method sources and the real B03 counts.

## Strongest attempted invalidations

1. **Marginal coverage is the wrong promise.** The Astra reviewer gave the explicit 95%-coverage, 100%-false-acceptance counterexample in `protocol/risk_analysis.md`. The [Jin–Candès paper](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v24/22-1176.html) treats selection error as distinct from ordinary conformal coverage. Resolution: no use of a 95% prediction interval as proof of 95% precision or individual safety. Selected-set error, automatic coverage and batch mass contamination have separate formulas.
2. **Even a flawless observed sample cannot support the desired bound.** The most optimistic nominal pool has 10 unflagged polyester positives, 6 unflagged cotton positives, or 12/7 including all flagged rows. None is an observed accepted set. At zero failures the one-sided 95% exact binomial upper limits would still be 25.9%, 39.3%, 22.1% or 34.8%, respectively. Fifty-nine independent error-free accepted units are needed for a 5% upper limit. Resolution: H02-1 is deferred; the current protocol is an exploratory composition-model comparison. A mathematically valid loose bound or all-review policy is not advertised as useful sorting.
3. **The source's 11 outliers are selected using the whole dataset.** The [OpenTextile methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12925456/) confirm repeated spectral-regression residual screening before its example PLS evaluation. Globally excluding them would cherry-pick model-concordant labels. Resolution: include all 68 nominally labeled pieces in the primary endpoint, stratify flags secondarily, and explicitly mark the published 5.18-point RMSE non-comparable to the new grouped all-label analysis.
4. **Grouping does not establish IID sampling.** Keeping two `sample_45` pieces together prevents a known leak, but supplier, garment lot and scan-session IDs are not available. [Official grouped-CV guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html) only guarantees the provided group does not cross folds; it cannot certify that unidentified dependence has vanished. Resolution: describe pooled outer results as internal and conditional on this collection, not external validation.
5. **No dedicated untouched final set exists.** B03 inspected all nominal composition rows to choose a feasible target. A new nominal train/validation/final split would have too few threshold positives for precision control and would not restore blinding that did not exist. Resolution: one frozen five-fold group allocation with four-fold inner grouped tuning; record that it is exploratory. Confirmation needs new audited independent data.
6. **Label error can reverse apparent risk.** Supplier proportions may systematically overstate a desired fibre. A nominally perfect router could be wrong relative to chemical assays. [Original noisy-label conformal analysis](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume25/23-1549/23-1549.pdf) requires explicit assumptions for clean-label statements. Resolution: restrict target to nominal supplier share, show hypothetical accepted-label-mistake sensitivity, and prohibit clean-label or industrial-risk claims.

## Alternatives considered

The Astra reviewer pointed to [Learn-then-Test](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.01052v5) as the closest existing precision-control alternative: it calibrates a fixed candidate family using valid risk tests with multiplicity handling under independent/exchangeable calibration and matching deployment. This is better matched to accepted-set risk than marginal interval coverage, but the current label quality and positive counts make a meaningful strict guarantee implausible. The [Jin–Candès selection method](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v24/22-1176.html) is another real prior method; its expected false-discovery control is a different claim from a realized batch's purity. A disjoint calibration/final allocation would further deplete positives. Ordinary grouped nested resampling is chosen for the narrower reproduction question because it uses all 67 labeled parent groups once for internal held-out prediction while keeping model selection within outer training.

The reviewers differed on whether nominal polyester percentage or the paper's cotton+wool+lyocell sum should be primary. Polyester has direct historical-proxy relevance and 34 distinct reported values in 68 labeled pieces, but the author-defined sum permits a more direct critique/reproduction of the published PLS workflow and avoids choosing a new target to chase threshold counts. The root froze the author-defined sum as primary **before any model result** and polyester/cotton MAE as secondary. This is not a claim that lyocell is botanically natural or that this sum matches any recycler rule. The serious simple comparator is a fold-training median; Ridge is a bounded secondary classical alternative. No neural network or cross-instrument pooling is justified here.
