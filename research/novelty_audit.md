# B02 novelty and nearest-method audit

As of 2026-09-25. This is a bounded prior-art review, not a world-wide novelty certificate. `research/prior_art_matrix.csv` records the exact overlap, label basis, independent unit and retrieval status for each item. Original pages or author papers were opened; restricted/abstract-only material is labelled accordingly. Source IDs are in `research/sources.jsonl`.

## Findings that invalidate a generic method claim

1. **Composition prediction is established.** The [OpenTextile-NIR authors (S50)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12925456/) already fit mean-spectrum PLS natural-fibre regression on the same 71 supplier-labelled samples, excluding 11 residual outliers. [Becker et al. (S41)](https://www.nilskroell.com/publication/becker_2024_nirtextiles/Becker_2024_NIRTextiles.pdf) studied NIR blend quantification and an industrial sorter trial. [Bonifazi et al. (S43)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12649-023-02413-z) used hierarchical SWIR PLS-DA and an explicit “Not classified” outcome. A 2006 [publisher abstract (S44)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1366/000370206777412194) also places quantitative cotton/polyester NIR well before this project; its full methods were inaccessible, so its metric is not used as a fair benchmark.
2. **The routing workflow is established.** The [T-REX technical guidance (S35), printed p. 8](https://circulartextiles.aalto.fi/trexproject/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/T-REX-Technical-Guidance-For-Recyclable-Garments.pdf) depicts non-reusable garments scanned by NIR and matched to recycler requirements. [Valvan (S30)](https://www.valvan.com/en/solutions/textile-sorting-recycling) publicly describes NIR fibre-concentration and colour sorting. These are workflow/vendor descriptions; they do not prove every implementation's reliability, but they defeat a first-in-kind workflow claim.
3. **Selective decisions and risk control are established.** [Jin and Candès (S39)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v24/22-1176.html) address selecting units with expected false-discovery proportion control. [Learn then Test (S15)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01052) addresses high-probability control of a specified risk with held-out calibration and multiple testing. [Conformal Risk Control (S16)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814) addresses expected monotone loss, a different quantity unless matched to the decision. [MAPIE's binary precision example (S40)](https://mapie.readthedocs.io/en/stable/generated/risk_control/1-quickstart/plot_risk_control_binary_classification/) already supplies calibrated threshold selection. Ordinary marginal conformal interval coverage cannot be renamed precision among accepted specimens.

No original algorithmic gap has been verified. A textile-specific **evaluation** may still be worthwhile if B03 finds suitable independent specimens and labels, and if a prespecified simple classical baseline can be meaningfully compared. A faithful reproduction/limitations study is the fallback. Neither implies environmental savings, industrial acceptance, novelty or contest readiness.

## Data choice is unsettled

The original [OpenTextile article (S50)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12925456/) supplies 71 physical pieces and supplier labels, including 11 author-flagged outliers; its file grouping and license still need B03 audit. [Stipanovic et al. (S42)](https://doi.org/10.3390/recycling10040152) appear to use chemical-reference cotton percentages and an independent 111-specimen set, making a stronger label comparator, but direct full text was not retrieved and public data availability was not confirmed. [NIST NIR-SORT 2.0 (S36–S38)](https://www.nist.gov/mml/mmsd/security-technologies-group/near-infrared-spectra-origin-defined-and-real-world-textiles) offers controlled blends and multiple instruments; actual files/license and threshold boundary counts are unaudited. [HyperTex (S51)](https://zenodo.org/records/21489250) lists 198 HSI captures but files are embargoed until 2026-09-30 and the displayed volume is 161.5 GB; it is not accessible for today's B03 audit.

B03 must compare these alternatives before preserving the initial OpenTextile choice. Known-provenance blends can support a controlled composition question but do not by themselves establish performance on mixed post-consumer garments. Any label derived from care tags or suppliers remains a proxy. No final-test data has been accessed or used for method selection.

## Claims prohibited pending later evidence

- No new NIR-plus-threshold, abstention, conformal risk-control or recycler-routing method is claimed.
- No guarantee of ≤5% false accepts, high industrial stream purity or compliance with a real recycler's current intake terms is claimed. For intuition only, zero errors in 20 independent accepted observations would still allow a one-sided 95% binomial upper bound around 13.9% for a **fixed** policy; choosing that policy on the same data voids the simple bound.
- No numerical prior-art result is imported into this project's result ledger. Published metrics are scoped to their own samples, labels, exclusions and split designs.

## Verification gaps

S38 full PDF was too large for the browser fetch; official NIST abstract/page was opened. Original [cfBH replication repository](https://github.com/ying531/selcf_paper) was opened and showed three commits but no visible license file; treat reuse rights as unverified. The [LTT repository](https://github.com/aangelopoulos/ltt) identifies an MIT license; [MAPIE](https://github.com/scikit-learn-contrib/MAPIE) identifies BSD-3-Clause and warns that v1 changed APIs. These are implementation options only after version and assumptions review; no code was installed or run. This search found no verified textile-specific selected-set risk-control evaluation on an audited open composition dataset, but absence from this bounded search is **not** evidence of world-first status.
