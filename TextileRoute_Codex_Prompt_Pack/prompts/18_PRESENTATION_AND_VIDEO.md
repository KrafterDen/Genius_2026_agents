## Work sequence

### Story and timing — 40–55 min
Write an English <=180-second application-video master with problem, measurement, AI contribution, real screen demonstration, evidence and limitations. Plan a separate <=5-minute final presentation, plus 4–5minute Q&A preparation under current international rules. National final format remains conditional until confirmed. No mandatory poster for AI. Keep exact rules in metadata.

### Actual assets and capture — 60–75 min
Build PPTX and PDF backup with readable real plots, profile/source attribution and transparent uncertainty. Capture the offline app executing actual sample spectra, including review/failure states, no fabricated output overlays. Prepare a local MP4 assembly, shot list, English narration script and SRT. Use existing legitimate fonts/assets; no music or footage with unclear rights.

### Voice and defense — 40–55 min
If actual authorized student narration exists, integrate it faithfully. Otherwise prepare a labelled silent/timing draft and a recording task. English narration is required, but the inspected rule does not explicitly require natural student voice: a transparent licensed synthetic alternative needs explicit user approval and a current rules check. Never call synthetic speech the student. Prepare at least20 evidence-based difficult questions covering spectra, weak labels, novelty, small sample size, decision risk, industrial requirements, environmental scope and personal work. Do not script false personal experience.

### Media and presentation validation — 40–55 min
Measure actual MP4 duration, subtitle alignment, resolution, audio availability and local playback. Rehearse slide timing and demo failure fallback. Verify all numbers and claims against the ledger and source permissions. Prepare correct national and international YouTube title/description text without publishing. Record final versus draft voice status.

## Independent web challenge
