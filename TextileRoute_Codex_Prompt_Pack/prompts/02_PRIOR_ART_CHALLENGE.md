Attempt to disprove the proposed novelty before investing in the custom method. The output must be a testable gap, a faithful reproduction opportunity or an honest finding that the initial idea is already covered.

## Work sequence

### Textile search and citation tracing — 55–65 min
Search composition regression, blended fabric NIR sorting, calibration transfer, open-set/rejection methods, recycler feedstock classification and label limitations. Follow citations and available code. Seek 10–20 genuinely close works across textile and general methods if possible, not a quota padded with irrelevant papers. Record exact searches and inaccessible texts. Compare actual tasks and metrics, not titles.

### Decision/uncertainty alternatives — 55–70 min
Independently inspect selective classifiers, existing binary precision control, learn-then-test and interval-based routing. Determine which proposed features already exist. Search whether process-specific eligibility, review queues, supplier-label uncertainty or multi-profile decisions have been evaluated before. A generic conformal reject option is an existing baseline, not our automatic contribution.

### Candidate hypotheses and cheap falsification — 40–55 min
Build a prior-art matrix with problem, labels, independence, metric, algorithm, code, limits and overlap. Propose at most three narrowly scoped candidate contributions. For each specify the exact additional component, closest comparator, falsifying result, data required and maximum initial pilot budget. Consider a robust evaluation/benchmark contribution if no new algorithmic gap survives; explain competition implications without promising medals.

### Decision and research ledger — 35–50 min
Compare the independent reviews, verify references and select a provisional direction plus one fallback within textile routing. Write why it is only provisional until data audit. Record novelty as unverified, incremental, reproduced or supported-by-survey, never world-first from absence of search results. Update the plan and prohibit premature claims in reports.

## Acceptance gates
1. Closest existing selective/reject/risk-control methods are represented, not omitted.
2. All cited papers are actually verified; missing full texts are marked.
3. Each candidate has a falsification test and a data prerequisite.
4. A concrete provisional choice or honest no-gap finding is recorded.

## Meaningful depth work after the core
Reproduce a small published toy example to understand a critical guarantee, inspect licensing and maintenance of closest implementations, and search non-English textile terms where useful.

## Stop, narrow or invalidate when necessary
If the idea already exists, revise its contribution or label the work as reproduction/evaluation. Never hide prior art or keep an unsupported novelty claim to make the project look stronger.
