## Work sequence

### Template refresh and section mapping — 35–50 min
Reopen the international AI page and paper PDF. Map every required section, including abstract <=250 words, student roles and external contributions. Current total limit20 pages includes cover and references. Do not copy the national15-body-page rule. Record current font/margin details and any changes.

### English writing and synchronization — 60–75 min
Create a complete clear English manuscript, preserving precise uncertainty, small-sample qualifications, environmental mechanism and exact novelty status. Use a common figure/result source; never manually retype numbers inconsistently. Ensure student-role descriptions truthfully distinguish agent assistance. Update related work only with verified material and do not add a new untested claim during translation.

### Supporting scientific assets — 45–60 min
Prepare Ukrainian/English short abstracts, plain-language summary, model/data cards, reproducibility guide, glossary, source index and a concise industrial-scope statement. Distinguish required organizer uploads from helpful supporting material. Generate English PDF and optionally editable DOCX with reproducible settings.

### Cross-language and citation audit — 40–55 min
Render all English pages, check total pages and template compliance, compare every headline/table with the Ukrainian report and ledger, validate references and hyperlinks, and ensure no citation is an invented author/title/DOI. Record conflict resolutions and pending human metadata. Finish with an international preparation checklist, not a claim of submission.
