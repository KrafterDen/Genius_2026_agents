Establish exactly what environmental decision is being improved and what the current organizer requires. Produce an evidence-backed requirements baseline and an honest scope; do not turn a plausible recycling story into a proven environmental effect.

## Work sequence

### Organizer audit — 40–50 min
Open current Ukraine 2027 home/category/participant pages and international AI category/template. Record exact stage, season, dates, page-count treatment, file size, formats, language, video, eligibility, student-work and final presentation requirements. Inspect PDF layout pages. Distinguish AI from Coding/Science and current from stale sections. Populate a machine-readable requirements registry with source locators and unresolved conflicts. Prepare an unsent organizer query only for truly unresolved issues.

### Recycler requirements and environmental chain — 65–80 min
Read JRC Table 5 and nearby qualifications, primary project reports and reachable current recycler specifications. Define at least two candidate fibre-composition profiles only where actually supported, including source date, material percentages, other conditions and whether historical/scenario/current. Seek actual operator evidence; record failure honestly. Explain why dominant-fibre classification can be insufficient without claiming that any 10% mixture universally ruins a batch. Separate reusable garments from end-of-life feedstock.

### Claim and submission contracts — 40–55 min
Write a precise problem statement, user workflow, intended measurement and unit of analysis. Populate claims.csv with measured/assumed/referenced distinctions. Convert the submission inventory into artifacts with validators and required/recommended/unresolved status. Prepare private participant fields without inventing values. Record uncertainty about Ukrainian enrollment while abroad rather than assuming eligibility.

### Independent challenge and integration — 40–55 min
Have the challenger ask whether a simple sorting rule or existing commercial approach already solves the stated problem, whether the proposed dataset measures the relevant criterion, and whether environmental language is justified. Resolve each objection with an evidence-backed scope change, upcoming test or explicit limitation. Update PROJECT.md and requirements snapshots.

## Acceptance gates
1. Every material rule and industrial claim has an opened primary source and exact scope.
2. Historical recycler thresholds are not described as universal/current rules.
3. National and international deliverables are separated, including unresolved human eligibility.
4. A narrower measurable problem survives the challenger or is explicitly marked conditional.

## Meaningful depth work after the core
Trace two example end-of-life items through alternative recycler profiles and all unobserved requirements; build boundary-case specification tests without inventing measured samples. Check source dates and archive allowed metadata/excerpts.

## Stop, narrow or invalidate when necessary
If current rules are conflicting, keep drafts conditional. If no current operator specification is reachable, use a clearly labelled historical/scenario profile, not invented industrial certification.
