## Work sequence

### Candidate narrowing — 40–55 min
Revisit up to three hypotheses from block02 against actual data. Examples to investigate, not mandates: review prioritization based on downstream routing mistakes and weak-label ambiguity; decisions robust to documented composition rounding; spatial measurement selection only if independent spatial data justifies it. State exactly what is added beyond a calibrated reject classifier. Reject candidates that require missing ground truth.

### Independent alternatives and micro-design — 40–55 min
Have a challenger freshly search the exact narrowed formulation and strongest baselines. Produce a diagram, equations/pseudocode, inputs/outputs, resource requirements and identifiable effect. A generic expected-utility score may already exist; cite it and define the actual added evaluation/contribution. Choose one primary candidate and one bounded fallback.

### Falsification pilots — 65–80 min
Implement the smallest functional pilot on development groups. Compare to random review, uncertainty review and relevant existing policy at the same review budget, if review allocation is the chosen task. Use an explicitly simulated label-reveal oracle rather than claiming expert-time savings. Test whether gains survive removal of the proposed component and sensible label uncertainty. Preserve neutral or negative results.

### Decision and student-owned component preparation — 35–50 min
Keep, modify once, or abandon the candidate based on evidence and prior art. Update the research question and success criteria before final freeze. Prepare a meaningful small experiment/component the student can understand, alter and interpret, with an explanation workbook. Do not record it as completed by the student. Update the contribution log with actual agent work.

## Acceptance gates
1. One concrete added component is distinguishable from existing baselines.
2. The pilot can falsify the proposed advantage and includes the closest comparator.
3. Claimed human savings match what was actually simulated/measured.
4. The chosen scope and pending genuine student role are explicit.

## Meaningful depth work after the core
Replicate pilot results with a second implementation or development grouping, examine item-level causes and cost-weight sensitivity, and refine the student learning notebook.

## Stop, narrow or invalidate when necessary
If the benefit vanishes or prior art covers it, report that. A reproducible comparison or data-limitation result is preferable to fabricated invention. Material project-scope changes are documented and batched for user awareness.
