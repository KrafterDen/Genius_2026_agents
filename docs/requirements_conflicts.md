# B01 requirements and source conflicts (2026-09-25)

1. **Ukraine 2027 vs old final text.** [The home page (S01)](https://ukraine.geniusolympiad.org/) announces the 2027 schedule but says category information is being updated and the national final format is undecided. [The AI category section (S02)](https://ukraine.geniusolympiad.org/categories.html) still describes the **2026** Kyiv final. The AI report/video numbers are therefore a published **working baseline** for draft preparation, not certified 2027 compliance. No 2027 AI final presentation duration is confirmed.
2. **Ukrainian eligibility for a participant abroad.** [S03](https://ukraine.geniusolympiad.org/participants.html) specifies grades 8–11 at Ukrainian general secondary institutions and age 13 at registration. It does not explicitly decide enrollment while physically abroad. The participant's actual enrollment/age/route are unknown; leave `eligible=false/unknown` in release planning until private evidence and, if needed, organizer clarification.
3. **AI template wording.** The [international AI page (S04)](https://geniusolympiad.org/disciplines.html) unambiguously requires an AI research paper. [The linked AI-specific PDF template (S05)](https://geniuscountries.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/GENIUS_AI_ResearchPaper.pdf) opens with a generic 'science projects' paper bullet. Apply the AI category rule; do not treat that bullet as exempting AI. The six-page template is instructional and says a submitted paper may have up to 20 pages including all sections.
4. **Missing stated caps.** Audited international AI page/template state no total paper word cap and no PDF byte cap; national AI page states no Word-file byte cap. Keep these fields `null`, not invented limits. Recheck the application portal before release.
5. **Other categories.** International Coding's Extreme Heat & Wildfires topic, public repository and 24-hour hackathon, international Science's poster, and Speech's AI environmental-cost topic do not transfer to AI. [S04 category sections](https://geniusolympiad.org/disciplines.html) explicitly route projects centered on developing/evaluating AI/ML to AI.
6. **Dataset provenance.** [S06 VTT abstract](https://cris.vtt.fi/en/publications/introducing-opentextile-nir-near-infrared-hyperspectral-imaging-a/) says 'post-industrial samples' and also 'used garments' from an end-of-life partner. Actual files and source metadata must decide the population in B03. Supplier composition labels are not independent chemical assays.
7. **Recycler threshold currency.** [S09 JRC Table 5](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/bitstream/JRC141441/JRC141441_01.pdf) explicitly calls its WRAP-derived classification a 2024 snapshot. [Infinited's public technology/FAQ pages (S28–S29)](https://infinitedfiber.com/our-technology/) discuss ~88% cotton preference but are undated and are not a lot-acceptance agreement. The WRAP database landing (S33) was reachable, but underlying Airtable records were not inspected. No universal current recycler threshold is established.
