# OpenTextile-NIR v1.0 — local use card

**Source and rights.** [Zenodo record 18269172](https://zenodo.org/records/18269172), DOI `10.5281/zenodo.18269172`, version 1.0, CC BY 4.0. Cite Sormunen, Mahlamäki, Satu-Marja Mäkelä and Mikko Mäkelä (2026) and the dataset DOI; cite the [data article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12925456/) for its methods. The checked record and six small files have matched source checksums in the local, gitignored `data/manifests/dataset_manifest.json`. Reuse must preserve attribution and mark adaptations.

**Population.** Approximately 10 cm square pieces cut from garments handled by a Finnish post-industrial textile operator, as described by the authors. There are 71 piece rows, 70 conservative parent-prefix groups, and no verified representative sampling frame for general textile waste. The two `sample_45` pieces share a parent prefix and must stay together. The exact number of distinct source garments and supplier, garment-lot or acquisition-session dependencies are not recoverable from the released small tables.

**Inputs.** One 288-band mean SWIR reflectance spectrum per piece, 953.04–2547.64 nm, with a rectangular per-piece ROI coordinate; mean RGB values are available only as visual context. Original SWIR cubes and RGB images were listed but not downloaded. The authors report dark/white reference conversion and dead-pixel removal. The local audit found no missing or nonfinite mean spectral cells and no empty ROI ranges. Mean spectra represent scanned surface regions, not entire garments.

**Labels.** Eleven fibre columns report nominal percentages supplied through the waste operator by waste suppliers/manufacturers. There are 57 unflagged rows with 100-point reported totals, 11 model-residual outlier-flagged rows with 100-point totals, and 3 rows marked unknown with no composition. These are **not chemical reference values**. A blank component in a nominal 100-point row is a component not reported in that supplier statement; it must not be advertised as laboratory-confirmed zero. Inexact reporting and garment wear leave unknown composition error. The outlier labels were derived from spectral-model residuals on the entire collection, so a blanket pre-evaluation exclusion would contaminate a prospective evaluation.

**Supported use now.** Source-scoped description of the released metadata and mean spectra; a future explicitly exploratory grouped reproduction of classical NIR composition modeling if such experimental work is authorized. The 2024 JRC cotton and polyester thresholds may be examined only as historical composition-proxy scenarios. Three unknown rows remain unlabeled; outliers form an uncertain stratum rather than confirmed bad data.

**Unsupported use.** No chemical truth, independent industrial trials, full recycler acceptance, whole-item purity, mass contamination, emission benefit, current universal threshold, fair/population-wide generalization or finite-sample false-acceptance guarantee follows from this collection. For 95% polyester the unflagged nominal class balance is only 10 eligible versus 47 ineligible pieces (10 versus 46 parent groups); for 80% cotton it is 6 versus 51 (6 versus 50 groups). These are capacity counts, not model outcomes.

**Storage and processing.** Raw files are local and gitignored under `data/raw/open_textile_zenodo_18269172/`. No derived spectra, splits, models or predictions exist as of B03. The exact audit command, hashes, composition/group statistics and NIST alternative status are in `protocol/data_audit.md`, `protocol/data_audit.json`, `protocol/grouping.json` and `protocol/target_feasibility.csv`. Recheck the record version and rights before any public redistribution.
