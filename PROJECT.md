# TextileRoute — current scope

Provisional B02 question: on audited independent physical textile groups with composition labels of known provenance, how do simple classical NIR composition estimation, direct eligibility classification and an existing REVIEW/precision policy compare on observed false acceptance and automatic coverage for a named historical fibre-composition proxy? This is a proposed **domain evaluation**, with no new algorithmic or formal-guarantee claim. B03 may find that no named threshold has enough independent cases to study. Supplier-label thresholds define a proxy target; they are not a deployable baseline unless those labels are available when sorting. This is a local research prototype for GENIUS AI, not a purity certificate.

Status after B02: current organizer pages and candidate recycler sources were audited in B01. Ukrainian 2027 AI details remain a working baseline because the organizer says updates are pending. JRC criteria are a 2024 snapshot; Infinited's public cotton preference is not an operator acceptance contract. Dataset files, license, individual label truth, independent grouping units, our baseline performance, calibration, environmental effects and participant contest eligibility are **unverified**. No project model has been trained. The VTT article describes 71 physical samples, which is not a verified evaluation sample count. Synthetic B00 fixtures have no scientific validation role.

B02 prior art found composition prediction, textile abstention, NIR-to-recycler routing and selected-set risk-control methods already established; see `research/novelty_audit.md`. B03 must compare OpenTextile against NIST NIR-SORT 2.0 and accessible alternatives, inspect actual files/license/groups and decide target feasibility. Scientific scope may narrow further or yield no defensible research gap. Outputs must distinguish surface NIR evidence from unobserved layers, finishes, trims, moisture and process conditions. Public claims will require the verified result ledger; drafts and release remain separate.
