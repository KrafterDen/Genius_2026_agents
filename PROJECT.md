# TextileRoute — current scope

Provisional B01 question: on audited independent physical sample groups with identifiable supplier composition labels, can a spectrum-based review policy improve automatic coverage at a stated false-acceptance rate against a named fibre-composition proxy target, relative to simple spectral baselines? The question is conditional on B02 prior art and B03 data feasibility. Supplier-label thresholds define the proxy target; they are not a deployable baseline unless those labels are available when sorting. This is a local research prototype for GENIUS AI, not a purity certificate or novelty claim.

Status after B01: current organizer pages and candidate recycler sources have been audited in `docs/requirements_current.json`, `docs/requirements_conflicts.md` and `research/problem_evidence.md`. Ukrainian 2027 AI details remain a working baseline because the organizer says updates are pending. JRC criteria are a 2024 snapshot; Infinited's public cotton preference is not an operator acceptance contract. Dataset files, license, labels, independent grouping units, baseline performance, calibration, environmental effects and individual contest eligibility are **unverified**. No model has been trained. The VTT article describes 71 physical samples, which is not a verified evaluation sample count. Synthetic B00 fixtures have no scientific validation role.

The next decision gate is B02 prior art, then B03 data and target feasibility. Scientific scope may narrow further or yield no defensible gap. Outputs must distinguish surface NIR evidence from unobserved layers, finishes, trims, moisture and process conditions. Public claims will require the verified result ledger; drafts and release remain separate.
